Energy & Power Innovation Centre (EPIC) at Mohawk College

What if your facility, product, or operations could run more efficiently, cleaner, and be positioned to scale with confidence?
Our IRAP-supported program offers organizations the opportunity to gain an outside expert perspective on their carbon footprint, operational efficiency, and power systems behind a facility, product, or platform.
The EPIC uses applied research and sustainability best practices to evaluate your emissions, energy use, and power systems performance, helping you reduce carbon emissions and system inefficiencies, validate your electrification strategies, and drive long-term, sustainable growth.
The program is funded by NRC-IRAP and primarily offered to their portfolio companies across Canada.

Depending on your needs and goals, you can now apply for one of our tailored streams:
Stream 1: Facility Scope 1, 2 & 3 Emissions Baselining 
Ideal for manufacturers and facility operators who need to establish a credible starting point for their carbon strategy. 
We focus on:
•	Quantifying Scope 1,2, and 3 emissions across your operations to establish an accurate baseline 
•	Identifying high-emitting sources and providing you with tool to track your progress over time

Stream 2: Product Carbon Footprint
Ideal for companies looking to understand the carbon footprint of their products to guide sustainability decision-making
We focus on:
•	Quantifying the carbon footprint of your product across its key life stages, from raw materials to end-of-life
•	Identifying emissions hot spots across a products lifecycle and recommending strategies to reduce emissions

INVESTMENT
The program offers approximately 120 hours of work. The expectation is that companies invest 6-8 hours of their time throughout the process.
For IRAP clients, participation in this project is funded by IRAP.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
Small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) must meet the following criteria to be considered for this service:
•	Minimum of one full-time employee receiving a T4,
•	Less than 500 employees,
•	The firm is operating in Canada and has a CRA Business Number,
•	The firm has demonstrated a desire to grow and generate profits,
•	The firm meets IRAP program eligibility criteria,
•	The firm has a sustainability goal related to EPIC’s expertise.
